# SOWPODS word list credit.

The bundled lexicon at `sowpods.txt` is the SOWPODS word list
(one word per line, UTF-8, LF line endings).

Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jesstess/Scrabble/master/scrabble/sowpods.txt

File format: one word per line, UTF-8 without BOM.

Note: the NASPA Word List (NWL) is proprietary and is not bundled here.
